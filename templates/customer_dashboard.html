Pull (Back, Lats & Biceps)

  • Incline Dumbbell Bicep Curls: 3 sets x 10 reps

Legs & Core Power

  • Barbell Back Squats: 4 sets x 6 reps
  • Romanian Deadlifts: 3 sets x 8-10 reps

  • Standing Calf Raises: 4 sets x 15 reps
